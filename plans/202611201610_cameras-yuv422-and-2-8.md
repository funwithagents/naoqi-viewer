# Camera display in YUV422, and on NAOqi 2.8

**Status:** Done

Implements [cameras.md](../specs/cameras.md) ("Colorspace: YUV422 on the wire, RGB in the picture" and "Subscriptions are the trap"): the camera source subscribes both cameras in YUV422, the cameras' own colorspace, and converts to RGB on the host, so a robot sends a third fewer bytes and converts nothing; and it clears its leftover subscriptions on NAOqi 2.8, whose `ALVideoDevice` has no `unsubscribeAllInstances`. Leaves out the resolution and rate knobs (cameras.md open question 1) and compressing the images on the robot.

## Scope

- `src/naoqi_viewer/cameras.py` — `DISPLAY_COLORSPACE = 9`; `yuv422_to_rgb`; `decode_images` takes `width × height × 2` bytes and converts; `_clear_instances` with `INSTANCES_PER_NAME`, the 2.8 fallback
- `tests/mock_naoqi.py` — `rgb_to_yuv422`; each subscription served in the colorspace it asked for; `unsubscribeAllInstances` only when the mock plays 2.1
- `tests/test_cameras.py` — tolerant pixel checks for YUV422's rounding; the decode's known colours and refusals; the 2.8 startup clearing
- `tests-e2e/test_cameras_live.py` — the pictures' colours against NAOqi's own RGB
- `specs/cameras.md`, `specs/_index.md`, `specs/_overview.md` — the colorspace, the 2.8 clearing, the summaries

## Steps

1. `yuv422_to_rgb(pixels, width, height)`: pairs `Y0 U Y1 V`, each pixel its own luma and its pair's colour, BT.601 at limited range in float32, rounded and clipped to a contiguous `(height, width, 3)` uint8 array.
2. `decode_images` skips an entry that is not `width × height × 2` bytes (or has an odd width) and converts the rest; `DISPLAY_COLORSPACE` becomes 9.
3. The mock encodes its RGB images to YUV422 for a subscription that asked for 9, and serves RGB otherwise; with `version="2.8…"` its `ALVideoDevice` has no `unsubscribeAllInstances`.
4. `_clear_instances`: `unsubscribeAllInstances` when the service has it, else `unsubscribe` of `naoqi_viewer_0` to `_6`.
5. Tests: the fast tier as in Scope; the live colour test.
